About fiha — the asthetic store

What started as a tiny hobby of crocheting gifts for friends turned into fiha — the asthetic store — a small, home-run gifting studio based in Lucknow, UP, India.

Every hamper is packed by hand, every piece curated with intention, every order treated like it's for someone we deeply care about. No factories, no mass production — just heart, craft, and a lot of love.

Our Founder

Mariyam Siddiqui is the founder and owner of fiha — the asthetic store. Since 2025, she has been curating handpicked gifts and aesthetic finds for 2000+ happy customers across India.

Return & Refund Policy

⚠️ Important: Unboxing Video is MandatoryWe only accept return claims for damaged products if an unboxing video is provided. Without it, no claim will be processed.

Eligibility

  • Returns are accepted only for damaged products
  • Customer must report within 3 days of delivery
  • Claim must be submitted on the same day the parcel was delivered
  • An unboxing video (recorded from before opening) is compulsory
  • Without an unboxing video, claims will not be accepted

How to Claim

1. Record an unboxing video before or while opening the package
2. WhatsApp us at +91 9580083101 on the day of delivery
3. Send the unboxing video + photos of the damage
4. Our team will review within 24–48 hours

Non-Returnable Items

  • Personalized / customized products
  • Items without unboxing video proof
  • Items reported after 3 days of delivery

Refunds

If approved, refunds are processed to the original UPI ID within 5–7 business days. COD orders receive UPI refund.
